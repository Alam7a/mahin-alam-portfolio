About

PhD student in Civil Engineering.

I am a PhD student at the University of Windsor, supervised by Dr. Yong Hoon Kim. I started the program in May 2026. My research is in intelligent transportation systems. I test and validate automated driving systems in simulation, and I use vehicle trajectory analytics for traffic corridor optimization.

Current projects

What I am working on now.

Active, 2026-27

Autonomous Vehicle Highway Test Schedule

Funded through NSERC Alliance and the Highway Infrastructure Innovation Funding Program (HIIFP), in partnership with the Ontario Ministry of Transportation. PI: Dr. Lucas Bruck. Co-PI: Dr. Yong Hoon Kim.

Objective
Develop a framework to test and evaluate automated driving systems on Ontario highways, including winter conditions.
Approach
Simulation-based scenario design and evaluation, using human driver performance as a benchmark.
Contribution
Researcher and Project Manager. I guide four undergraduate research assistants and one MASc student.
In progress, Year 1

Trajectory-based traffic corridor optimization

My PhD research, supervised by Dr. Yong Hoon Kim.

Objective
Improve the operation of traffic corridors using vehicle trajectory data.
Approach
Vehicle trajectory analytics and modeling.
Contribution
Lead researcher on this topic.
